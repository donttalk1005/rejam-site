Google ma'lumotlaridan foydalanish

«Google bilan ulash» tugmasini bossangiz, Rejam faqat calendar.events ruxsatini so'raydi va kun tartibi, soatli vazifalar va qarz muddatlarini kalendaringizga voqea sifatida qo'shadi va yangilaydi. Kalendaringizdagi boshqa voqealarni o'qimaydi, saqlamaydi, hech kimga bermaydi. Batafsil: Maxfiylik siyosati.

How Rejam uses Google user data

If you press "Connect Google", Rejam requests only the calendar.events scope and uses it to create and update events for your schedule items, timed tasks and debt due dates. It does not read, store or share other events in your calendar. Details: Privacy Policy.
