Maxfiylik siyosati

Rejam — Telegram'dagi moliya va rejalashtirish yordamchisi (@RejamUz_bot). Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 10-noyabr.

Qanday ma'lumotlar yig'iladi

Telegram ID, ism, foydalanuvchi nomi va siz ixtiyoriy yuborgan telefon raqami; botga kiritgan kirim-chiqim, vazifa, odat, qarz va kun tartib yozuvlaringiz.

Ma'lumotlar qayerda saqlanadi

Yozuvlaringiz sizning o'z Google jadvalingizda saqlanadi. Bot xizmat uchun zarur minimal ma'lumotni (obuna, sozlamalar, balans tarixi) o'z serverida saqlaydi. Botga yuborgan xabarlaringizning nusxasi serverda saqlanmaydi.

Google Calendar ruxsati

«Google bilan ulash» orqali ruxsat bersangiz, bot faqat calendar.events ruxsatidan foydalanadi: kun tartib bandlari, soatli vazifalar va qarz muddatlarini kalendaringizga voqea sifatida qo'shadi va yangilaydi. Bot kalendaringizdagi boshqa voqealarni o'qimaydi, saqlamaydi va hech kimga bermaydi. Saqlanadigan yagona narsa — ulanishni davom ettirish uchun Google bergan yangilash tokeni.

Rejam Google API'laridan olingan ma'lumotlardan foydalanishi va boshqa ilovalarga uzatishi Google API Services User Data Policy talablariga, jumladan Limited Use talablariga mos keladi. Ma'lumotlar reklama uchun ishlatilmaydi, sotilmaydi va sun'iy intellekt modellarini o'qitish uchun berilmaydi.

Telegram Business

Botni Telegram Business orqali ulasangiz, mijozlaringiz bilan yozishmalar («o'chirilgan xabarlar» va «kotib» funksiyalari uchun) serverda 30 kun saqlanadi va keyin avtomatik o'chiriladi. Ulanishni uzganingizda ular darhol o'chiriladi.

Hisobot rasmlari

Grafikli hisobotlar QuickChart xizmati orqali chiziladi: unga faqat chizish uchun zarur raqamlar, kategoriya va yozuv nomlari yuboriladi, Telegram ID yoki ismingiz yuborilmaydi.

Sun'iy intellekt

Matn yoki ovozli xabarlarni tushunish, chek rasmlarini o'qish va maslahat berish uchun so'rovingiz matni, ovozli xabar yoki chek rasmi Google Gemini xizmatiga yuboriladi. Maslahat uchun kirim-chiqimlaringizning jamlangan ko'rsatkichlari ham yuboriladi. Google Calendar ma'lumotlari AI'ga yuborilmaydi.

Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Kalendar ruxsatini istalgan vaqtda botda (⚙️ Sozlamalar → 📅 Google Calendar → 🔌 Uzish) yoki Google hisobingiz sozlamalarida bekor qilishingiz mumkin. Barcha ma'lumotlaringizni o'chirish uchun uzplatformdev@gmail.com ga yozing yoki botda administratorga murojaat qiling.

Privacy Policy

Rejam is a personal finance and planning assistant in Telegram (@RejamUz_bot). Effective date: November 10, 2026.

What we collect

Your Telegram ID, name, username and an optional phone number you choose to share; the income, expense, task, habit, debt and schedule entries you send to the bot.

Where data is stored

Your entries are stored in your own Google Sheet. The bot keeps only the minimum needed to run the service on its own server (subscription, settings, balance history). Copies of the messages you send to the bot are not stored on the server.

Google Calendar access

If you choose "Connect Google", the bot uses only the calendar.events scope: it creates and updates events for your schedule items, timed tasks and debt due dates. It does not read, store or share other events in your calendar. The only thing stored is the refresh token Google issues so the connection keeps working.

Rejam's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI models.

Telegram Business

If you connect the bot through Telegram Business, conversations with your customers (for the "deleted messages" and "secretary" features) are kept on the server for 30 days and then deleted automatically. They are deleted immediately when you disconnect.

Report images

Charts are rendered by QuickChart: it receives only the numbers, category and entry names needed to draw the chart, never your Telegram ID or name.

Artificial intelligence

To understand text and voice messages, read receipt photos and give advice, your message text, voice message or receipt photo is sent to the Google Gemini service. Aggregated totals of your records may also be sent to produce advice. Google Calendar data is never sent to AI.

Deleting your data

You can revoke calendar access at any time in the bot (⚙️ Settings → 📅 Google Calendar → 🔌 Disconnect) or in your Google Account permissions. To delete all your data, email uzplatformdev@gmail.com or contact the administrator in the bot.
